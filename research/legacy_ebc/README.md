# Historical EBC workflows

Preserved source from the EBC entry consolidation; these files are not active
root entrypoints. `ebc_video_sep.py` and `ebc_tuning_video.py` retain their old
separate-panel and tuning displays. The old circle entry and renderer sources
and tests are included for historical comparison, not as a second maintained
video pipeline. They may require their contemporaneous dependencies/imports.

Active entries: `ebc_video_rectangle.py`, `ebc_video_circle.py` at the repository
root. See `docs/ebc_videos.md` for explicit configuration and shared structures.
